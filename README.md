# Sales & Inventory Management Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

An interactive Excel Business Intelligence project designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, product performance, and inventory conditions.

The project transforms transactional sales and operational data into an executive dashboard with interactive filtering, KPI monitoring, inventory alerts, and management insights.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor sales performance
- Analyze gross profit and profit margin
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare regional and category performance
- Analyze salesperson performance
- Monitor inventory levels
- Identify products requiring replenishment
- Support management decision-making

## Tools & Technologies
